# WP12 Site Photo Report: the pilot starts on 13 October

*12 October 2026 · WPN*

From Tuesday 13 October, a small group from the Consultant Supervision Team (CST) and the Works Package Contractor (WPC) will make their daily site photo reports in a new web app, the **WP12 Site Photo Report**, for two weeks. If the pilot goes well, the app becomes the standard way to report site photos on WP12 from Wednesday 11 November.

<p><img src="../screenshot-report.png" alt="A PDF report page: four captioned photos with chainage, activity, BQ ref and status" width="420"></p>

## Why we built it

Most site photos on WP12 reach us as loose images in WhatsApp groups. They are hard to caption one by one, they lose their context when forwarded, and after a while many can no longer be downloaded. Finding the photos for an RFI or a chainage a few weeks later means scrolling through chats.

The app keeps each photo with what it shows: the activity and its BQ item, the chainage and side, the structure, berm level or layer, the RFI no. and a status. It then turns the day's photos into a report that can be shared in the same WhatsApp groups and kept on file.

## What the app does

- **Tags each photo** with an activity from the contract BQ (143 activities) or one of the 50 contract QC tests, with the chainage filled in from the phone's GPS where the photo has a location.
- **Makes a PDF report** with the JKR and WPC logos, 4 or 2 photos per A4 page, and captions in English, BM or both.
- **Prints the caption on each photo**, so it keeps its details when it is forwarded.
- **Makes an RFI photo record** for each RFI, set out to go with the JKR Sabah RFI form, with a photo index and sign-off blocks for WPC (prepared by) and CST (checked by).
- **Keeps a register** of every report on the phone, which can be searched and exported to Excel.
- **For WPC, to try during the pilot:** the next-day RFI summary list, pasted from Excel or typed in, shared as a PDF in the same layout as the Excel list.

It runs in the phone's browser on iPhone and Android, and on a laptop. There is no app store download, no login and no server: the photos and records stay on the phone that made them.

## The pilot

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **When** | Tuesday 13 to Monday 26 October 2026 (two weeks) |
| **Who** | A small group of CST and WPC staff who report from site every day |
| **What they do** | Make each day's photo report in the app and post the PDF in the WhatsApp group instead of loose photos. Inspection photos go out as RFI photo records. |
| **Feedback** | In the existing WP12 RFI coordination WhatsApp group, using **Send feedback** in the app's Setup screen |
| **Briefing** | Monday 12 October at the site office: everyone installs the app and makes a test report |

We will judge the pilot on a few simple things: whether each person reports on at least 8 of the 10 working days, whether any data is lost, and whether anything stops a report being made. We will also ask how long a report takes; the aim is under 15 minutes during the pilot and under 10 at go-live.

## What could come next

Features are added only when users ask for them. Ideas already on the list include a report type (daily progress, inspection, site observation, site visit), mark-up on photos, favourite activities, weather by time period, and photo summaries for the monthly progress report. The app is also built so other work packages can use it with their own setup. The full list is in the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).
