# From site photos to an RFI photo record: a worked example

*22 October 2026 · WPN*

An RFI (Request for Inspection) is only as good as the evidence attached to it. The inspector wants to see what was built, where, and what it was built against. The RFI photo record in the WP12 Site Photo Report puts that on a few pages: the details, a photo index, the photos with their captions, the drawing references and the sign-off blocks.

This post follows one made-up inspection from the day before to the signed form. The names, numbers and chainages are samples. Use your own.

## The scenario

Sample user: a site engineer of team WPC, with an RFI to raise for the **berm drain concreting** on the right-hand side, **CH 70+120 to 70+300**. The RFI no. is **DR-301**, so the form will read *JKR-RFI-WP12-DR-301*. The inspection is planned for the next morning. The photo record has to be ready before the inspector arrives, and it must show the formwork, the steel, the levels and the line of the drain.

## Step 1. Prepare before going to site

- Open the app once with signal, so it updates itself and loads the project setup.
- In **Report details**, check your team, name, designation and initials.
- Make sure the phone's camera has location on. The GPS fills in the chainage and side only for photos taken with **Take photo** in the app or with the phone camera. A photo saved from WhatsApp has lost both.
- Decide the list of photos you need (a sample list below). Six to ten photos are enough for most RFIs; the point is that each one shows one thing.

| # | What to photograph | Why the inspector wants it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Wide view of the stretch, from one end | Shows the location and the extent |
| 2 | Formwork, with a tape against the width | Dimensions and alignment |
| 3 | Reinforcement, with a tape against the spacing | Bar size and spacing |
| 4 | Cover blocks in place | Concrete cover |
| 5 | Levels or string line along the drain | Gradient and line |
| 6 | The joint or the end stop | Workmanship at the details |
| 7 | The cleaned area before pouring | Readiness for concrete |

## Step 2. Take the photos and tag them

Take the photos in the app. For each one, the card shows the chainage and side from the GPS. Then tag:

1. **Activity:** pick the BQ item, e.g. *Berm drain – concreting*. The BQ ref prints with it.
2. **Chainage to:** tap **+ Chainage to** and type the end, e.g. *70+300*, so the caption reads as a stretch.
3. **Element:** type what the photo shows, e.g. *Berm 3, formwork*.
4. **RFI no.:** type **DR-301**. Several photos can share one RFI no.
5. **Status:** **Ready for inspection**. This is the status the photo index shows, so do not leave it blank.
6. **Drawing reference** (experimental): pick the drawing, e.g. *D1 WD/JKR/WP12/CS/DR/PNP/002*. It will be listed at the end of the record.

Tips that save time: after the first photo, tap **Same as previous** on the next one; or select all seven and use **Tag selected**, then fix only the element on each. Do not forget the photos you took before you thought of the RFI: they can be tagged later in the same way.

Once any photo has an RFI no., the main screen shows an **RFI card** that counts the RFIs and the photos tagged to each. It is the quick check that none was left out.

## Step 3. Make the RFI photo record

1. Tap **Generate report**.
2. Near the top you will see **RFI photo records**. Tap **Make RFI photo records**.
3. Open **DR-301**. The panel is already filled from the photos: zone, chainage range and side, the activities with their BQ refs, and the number of photos.
4. **Description of works:** leave it to use the activities, or type the wording of the RFI form, for example *Concreting of berm drain, RHS, CH 70+120 to 70+300*.
5. **Comments:** anything the inspector should know, for example *Concrete grade and mix to be confirmed at the batching plant. Levels checked by the surveyor on the morning of the inspection.* Keep it factual; it prints on the record.
6. If you ticked **Include alignment diagram** in the report options, the record shows the alignment line on page 1 too.

## Step 4. Preview, then save or share

Tap **Preview** and look at it as the inspector would:

- Does page 1 name the right RFI no., zone and chainage range?
- Does the photo index list every photo with its time and status?
- Is any photo upside down, blurred or the wrong one? Go back, fix the card, and make the record again.

Then choose:

| Button | What it does | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Save PDF** | Saves the PDF to the phone | You will attach it to the RFI form or print it |
| **Share PDF** | Opens the phone's share sheet | You send it straight to the office on WhatsApp or email |
| **Save Word** / **Share Word** | The same record as an editable .docx | The RFI form needs a different wording or layout |

**Save** and **Share** are separate on purpose: Save always puts a file on the phone, whichever browser you use.

## Step 5. The outcome

The record that goes with the RFI form has:

- **Page 1:** the RFI details, the photo index and the comments, with **Prepared by (WPC)** and **Checked by (CST)** blocks. Print it, and sign on paper.
- **Following pages:** the photos with their captions, two to a page by default. Each caption shows the date and time, the team, the chainage and side, the activity and BQ ref, the RFI no., the drawing and the status.
- **Last page:** the drawing references for the RFI's photos, and a small QR code for sharing the app.

Attach it to the RFI form and send it. On the morning of the inspection, the inspector already knows the location, what is ready, and what to look at.

After the inspection, update the photos' status for the next report: **Inspected** if it passed, **Rectification required** or **Re-inspection required** if not. The new photos taken after rectification keep the same RFI no., so the next record shows the whole history in one place.

## Step 6. Keep the record

- The **Register** (*RFIs* view) lists every RFI photo record you saved, with the status of its photos. Search for *DR-301* to find it again, and **Export CSV** to keep a copy.
- **Save ZIP** on the report screen keeps the PDF, the captioned photos, the RFI photo records and a CSV photo log in one file, for the RFI folder on the office computer.
- Back up the register: it lives only in the phone's browser.

## A short checklist

- [ ] Photos taken in the app, with location on
- [ ] Activity, element, chainage (and *to* for a stretch)
- [ ] The same RFI no. on every photo of the inspection
- [ ] Status **Ready for inspection**
- [ ] Drawing reference attached
- [ ] Description of works and comments checked
- [ ] Preview looked at before saving
- [ ] **Save PDF** (or **Share PDF**), then sign on paper
