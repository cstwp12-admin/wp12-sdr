# Change log

All changes to the WP12 Site Photo Report app. The version in use is shown in the app under **Setup** (bottom panel).

| Version | Date | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1.2.3 | 11 Oct 2026 | Pilot version. **Photos per page**: choose 2 (larger) or 4 per A4 page, for the daily report and the RFI photo record. **Status** for each photo (In progress, Completed, Ready for inspection, Inspected, Re-inspection required, Rectification required, Observation, or your own), printed in English and BM and kept in the register. **Level / element** box moved onto the photo card (typed, e.g. *Berm 3*, *Layer 2*, *P2*). **RFI no.** moved onto the photo card; the Generate screen shows a *Make RFI photo records* button at the top when photos have an RFI no. **Same as previous** now copies remarks and status, and keeps a photo's own GPS chainage. **Check before you share** lists photos with no activity or chainage, with buttons to go to each. **Send feedback on WhatsApp** and **User guide** in Setup. User guide added as GUIDE.md, and a news post on the pilot in posts/. The app asks the browser to keep its data and reminds you to export the register after 7 days. A short notice shows what changed after each update. **Alignment diagram (experimental, off unless ticked)**: a line from the start to the end chainage with the zones and a marker for each photo (above the line LHS, on it CL, below it RHS), with the chainage range covered, on page 1 of the daily report and of each RFI photo record. Optional **structure lines** under the diagram, each ticked on its own: Bridges, Pipe culverts, Box culverts, Junction culverts and RS walls, with a tick and the structure no. at each drawing chainage. **Chainage to** sits beside Chainage: tap **+ Chainage to** for a stretch of work. **Your own zones** in Setup: add, remove and rename zones (e.g. 5 zones for CST); a team setup update keeps them, and **Use team zones** goes back. **Sort by chainage** beside Sort by time. **RFI inspection list (experimental, for WPC)**: the next-day RFI summary list, pasted from Excel (a chainage location goes into the chainage boxes) or added one by one, sorted by time or by RFI no., shared as an A4 landscape PDF, WhatsApp text or CSV, with Clear list and earlier lists to open or delete; RFI nos. on photo cards are suggested from the list and the RFI photo record takes its description. Setup → *Experimental features* shows or hides it. **See what changed** link in the update notice and **What changed** in Setup. **RFI photo record simplified** as an attachment to the RFI form: drawing reference, stage, result boxes and the SOR block removed; activities with BQ / QC refs, structure / level and a **photo index** (no., time, activity, location, level, status) added; sign-off by **Prepared by (WPC)** and **Checked by (CST)**. Register and team sheet: the RFI *Stage* and *Result* columns replaced by *Photo status*. Fixed: summary table headings overlapping in bilingual reports; long header values (e.g. several zones) cut off, now shortened, e.g. *CST Z1, Z3, Z4*; zones listed in photo order instead of zone order; "BQ BQ" in the Register photo list. README: CST is the Consultant Supervision Team. |

## How a revision is recorded

1. The version number in the app (`index.html`) goes up for every change: a third number for small additions or fixes between planned releases (1.2 → 1.2.1), the second number for a planned release in the roadmap (1.2 → 1.3), and the first number for a major change (2.0).
2. A row is added at the top of the table above.
3. The GitHub commit message starts with the version, e.g. `v1.2 – RFI record: Inspected by (CST)`.
