What changed

Every version, newest first

All changes to the WP12 Site Photo Report app. The version in use is shown in the app under Setup (bottom panel).

Version 1.2.7

22 Oct 2026Latest
  • Bundle after the pilot started.
  • HEIC photos: when a HEIC photo cannot be converted the message now says why (the converter could not be loaded, or this kind of HEIC cannot be read) instead of always blaming the signal; a file that is really a JPEG with a .heic name is opened as it is; the converter is saved on the phone when the app installs, so a HEIC photo can be added without signal.
  • Scan a document or page is on by default (Setup → Experimental features switches it off) and the app now finds the page by itself: the four corners are placed on the page when it stands out from what is behind it, and you can still drag them; Rotate, Find the page again and Retake photo; Next shows the straightened page with a choice of look (Clean keeps colour and detail and evens out the light, Grey, Black and white, Original) and Brightness and Contrast sliders, then Add to report (or Adjust corners).
  • Photo cards: coloured chips Needs activity and Needs location show what still blocks a photo.
  • Photo list: Select all, Sort by time, Sort by chainage and New report sit in one tidy row; Collapse all stays by the heading.
  • Chainage: the boxes show the number keypad, and typing 71775 becomes 71+775 at once; Chainage to shows only when you tap + Chainage to, on the photo card, Quick post, Tag selected photos and the RFI request / Add RFI / Edit RFI sheet, which now has the same On the alignment | Elsewhere buttons as the photo card; the button lines up with the Chainage box.
  • Screen theme button in the top bar: Auto, Dark, Light and Sunlight (black on white with heavy borders, for reading outdoors).
  • RFI request: a request still waiting can be edited (Edit in My requests): the same ticket no., a revision mark (Rev. 1), and the list keeper's phone takes the new details when it scans or opens it.
  • Save and Share are separate buttons for the report PDF, captioned photos, RFI photo records and the RFI list (before, on a phone, Save PDF opened the share menu and never saved); the preview screens have a Save button beside Share.
  • RFI inspection list: two steps (RFIs, Share) instead of three; the PDF comes first, Layout and options and the optional Visual board fold underneath.
  • RFI board redesigned: light, clean cards under a dark header, with an alignment card.
  • Report output is grouped: Page layout, then Save or share.
  • RFI photo records are easier to find: a line in the empty main screen, and a card on the main screen (with a Make RFI photo records button once photos carry an RFI no.).
  • RFI request link is about a third shorter (a compact text form; old links still open).
  • Credit reads "WPN" only. A worked-example post, From site photos to an RFI photo record (posts/), follows one RFI from preparation to the signed form.
  • Screenshots in the README and guide were rebuilt from censored example photos (tools/make_screenshots.py).
  • Top bar on small phones no longer crowds the title.
  • Licences: LICENSE, THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md and Setup → About and licences list the open-source parts and their licences (the HEIC converter contains LGPL parts).
  • Documentation site rebuilt as plain pages with search, a contents list and a dark mode (the docs folder, built from the .md files; the earlier Jekyll theme is removed).

Version 1.2.6

15 Oct 2026
  • Pilot update.
  • Quick post: a button at the top of the main screen opens the camera; tag the photo on one screen (GPS chainage, side, recent activities as chips, status, remarks; structure, element and RFI no. under More) and Share to WhatsApp. Caption below the photo as a table (default; the photo itself is not covered), on the photo, or text only (the photo as taken, the caption as the message). Choose what the caption shows (date and time, team, your name, location, activity, BQ / QC ref., RFI no., status, remarks, location strip, app link). The photos also go into the day's report. The same caption choices and the below the photo style are on the report screen for Captioned photos.
  • Scan: a new button at the top reads QR codes with the camera, from a saved image (e.g. received on WhatsApp) or from a pasted message or link, and Load from a PDF reads an RFI list or report PDF made by the app; links tapped in WhatsApp open the app at the same place.
  • RFI request ticket (RFI inspection list → Request an inspection): a late request is made as a ticket image with a QR code and a message with a link; the person keeping the list scans it and accepts it (added to the list, marked Late request, with its remarks) or declines it with a reason, and the app makes a reply ticket for the requester. A request is also confirmed when the requester loads an RFI list that has its ticket no.; one left off a list for its date shows Not on the list, and one with no reply after 3 hours or on the day shows No reply yet with Remind.
  • RFI inspection list: Remarks for each RFI (own column in the PDF and Word); List options at the top with the PDF layout (Table as WPC's Excel list, or Schedule: time order, morning and afternoon, the time in large type, a location strip for each RFI) and structure lines for the line diagram; Share board: the list as a departures board in time order, up to 8 RFIs per page (each page at most 1600 px tall, so WhatsApp keeps it sharp), with an alignment strip down the right marking each list no. at its chainage; Board PDF; a revision no. when the list is shared again after changes. The whole list travels inside the RFI list PDF and the board PDF and in the link in the message, not in QR codes (a phone camera cannot read a long list from a code).
  • Today's RFIs: an RFI list loaded on an inspector's phone (tap the link, Scan → Load from a PDF, or paste the message) shows on the main screen in time order with a countdown, Start record (take the photos already tagged with the RFI no., activity, structure and chainage from the list) and Inspected.
  • Report record: page 1 of the PDF report carries the report record QR code (orange frame) and the Get the app QR code (no longer in the last page footer of the report); another phone scans the record into its Register (the code holds only the ref. no., name, number of photos and chainages, so a phone camera reads it), and Register → Import PDF reads every photo's details from the PDF itself.
  • CST sections line: CST-S1 to CST-S5 (62+700, 65+660, 68+620, 71+580, 74+540, 77+508) under the location strip, with CST structures for photos at a structure; on for team CST; the sections can be edited in Setup → Project (names, chainages, the structures tag), with Use project sections to go back.
  • Other references: memos, specifications, letters, method statements and other documents in the drawing library (choose the type), numbered M1, S1, L1 … in captions and on the References pages.
  • Snippet editor zoom: pinch with two fingers, + / −, Fit, and Move to drag around a zoomed drawing or document before boxing the part to keep. The Used on photos line and the note under the snippets now read for drawings and documents.
  • App link on a caption prints the app's address at the end of the caption (Quick post and captioned photos). A request still waiting can be withdrawn; handled requests can be removed.
  • Pilot round 2: Quick post opens your gallery (for photos taken with the phone's camera app; the in-app camera is a button, and Setup can change what opens), activity and structure are chosen in drawers, and the location has Chainage to and Elsewhere with a place (lab, office) like the photo card.
  • Photos taken in the app keep their camera original: Save to phone is offered after each photo (Android: Downloads; iPhone: share menu, Save Image), Setup → Photos and Quick post can save at once or stop asking, and Save ZIP can include the originals.
  • RFI inspection list is now three steps (RFIs, Layout, Share); a new Schedule, 2 columns layout for long lists; Preview the board before sharing; Requests is a fold-out and the scanner sheet is simpler (paste the link first; camera on request).
  • Audit fixes: the app tells you when the phone refuses to store something (full storage), the app opens from its saved copy after 4 seconds on a weak signal, and the QR reader is saved for offline use.
  • Pilot round 3: Quick post does nothing when opened (choose Gallery or Take photo; Setup can change that), has a Settings › link to Photos and Quick post, shows + Chainage to only when tapped (× Remove takes it away) and lines it up under Chainage (from); structure and status can be typed (Other…) as on the photo card; photos taken in the app are saved to the phone at once by default.
  • Requests moved to the top of RFI list step 1.
  • Zone colours (Schedule, Schedule 2 columns, board and the app list): a coloured stripe and legend by zone, switch Colour by zone.
  • Photos can be collapsed like the Claimpack lines: Collapse on a photo, Collapse all / Expand all in the list header.
  • Pilot round 4: the top bar on a phone shows the whole title (two lines) instead of cutting it; collapsed photos use the full width; the QR codes on page 1 of the PDF report moved to the footer corners (Scan for the app at the bottom right, the report record at the bottom left, which is off by default; the photo log is in the PDF itself, Register → Import PDF); the CST sections line is blue-grey and thin so it is not mistaken for the project's zone bar; documentation site: the guide, roadmap and change list as web pages with a modern theme (the docs folder, built from the .md files).
  • Scan a document or page (experimental, off by default; Setup → Experimental features): a button beside Take photo and a Scan page tile in Quick post; take a photo of a page, drag the four corner handles onto it, and the app straightens it into a flat page, black and white like a scan or in colour; Use the whole photo skips the straightening. The page goes in as a normal photo marked Scanned page (kept at up to 2400 px so print stays readable). Corners are placed by hand; automatic corner finding is planned for v1.3.

Version 1.2.5

13 Oct 2026
  • Preview before sharing: Preview on the report screen, on each RFI photo record and on the RFI inspection list shows the PDF page by page in the app, with Share / Save below it.
  • Word document (experimental): Save Word saves the report, an RFI photo record or the RFI inspection list as an editable .docx set out like the PDF (the same header with logos, title and detail boxes on every page, tables, photos 2 × 2 or 2 per page with their numbers and captions, drawing references, sign-off lines, page numbers and the credit line). The Word file is always saved to the phone (Downloads or Files), not shared, with Save again in the message bar if nothing appeared; open it in Word or WPS, edit, and send it from there.
  • Sharing after a slow build: when a PDF takes a few seconds to make, some phones no longer allow the share; the message bar at the bottom now says … is ready with a Share button to send it (report, RFI records, RFI list, preview). Errors while making a file now show in the message bar.
  • Drawing references (experimental): attach a snippet of a drawing (layout plan, structure detail, schedule) to a photo. On the photo card, + Drawing ref opens the drawing library on the phone; + Add a drawing snippet takes a PDF drawing (choose the page; opened on the phone with a PDF viewer that downloads the first time) or an image (JPG, PNG, HEIC, screenshot), lets you drag a box over the part to keep, and asks for the drawing no. (the usual start WD/JKR/WP12/CS/ is filled in and can be changed, recent trades such as DR/PNP/ are one tap; a drawing no. in the file name is picked up), rev., title, what the snippet shows, and optionally a structure and chainage range (filled in from a title such as … FROM CH. 63400.000 TO CH. 64100.000). Drawings with the same structure or a matching chainage are suggested on the photo card for one-tap attaching. The PDF report and the RFI photo record show Drawing: D1 WD/JKR/WP12/… in the photo caption and print each snippet on Drawing reference pages at the end, with the photos that refer to it: a wide snippet (plan, schedule) takes the full width, two upright snippets (sections, details) share a row.
  • Drawing pages on the report screen: Auto, 1 per page (fit to the page; a wide drawing gets a landscape page), 2 per page or 4 per page.
  • Scan with camera: take a photo of a paper drawing, drag the 4 corners onto the sheet and tap Straighten (an A-size sheet is set to its exact shape); Enhance gives a clean white background with dark lines. Captioned photos carry the drawing no. Tag selected can attach drawings to several photos. Setup → Experimental features shows or hides it and opens the library. Register and CSV: new column Drawing refs at the end.
  • Credit line: PDF pages carry a small line Generated with WP12 Site Photo Report v1.2.5 under the footer, the PDF file properties name the app, and captioned photos carry it in their file data (not on the picture).
  • 2 photos per page is now the default for everyone (larger photos; 4 per page is still one tap away), and the choice is shown as two page pictures with the page count for each.
  • Report ref. no. fix: each report on a phone now gets its own no. A no. counts as used once it is shown on the report screen, so New report always gives the next one, even if the last draft was never shared; and when the photos of a report that was already shared or saved are changed (e.g. removed and replaced with the next activity's photos), the next report gets the next no. and its own Register line (before, it kept the same no. and replaced the earlier Register line). Editing captions or details keeps the no.
  • Share this app: Setup shows a QR code of the app's address, with Share link and Copy link.
  • QR code on PDFs: a small QR code to the app at the right end of the footer on the last page of the report, RFI photo records and the RFI list (Scan for the app); Setup can switch it off.
  • Team logos from the team setup: team CST gets the CST logo on the right, WPC and SOR keep the setup logos; a logo chosen on the phone still comes first.
  • Share Word beside Save Word (report, RFI photo records, RFI list) on phones that can share files.
  • Captioned photos: Preview to see them before sharing, and tick boxes for the photo no. (now off by default) and the team on the first caption line.
  • Alignment diagram: the total length (14.81 km) and the length of each zone above the line.
  • Location strip on each photo: a small line of the whole alignment with the zones, the photo's zone stronger and a marker at its chainage, in the PDF and on captioned photos (not in Word). On by default under the caption; Top of the photo or Off on the report screen.
  • RFI inspection list line diagram (experimental): tick Include a line diagram to show each RFI's location along the alignment, marked with its list no. and time of inspection, above the table; RFIs elsewhere (e.g. the site laboratory) are listed under it.
  • Same photo twice: adding a photo that is already in the report is skipped, with a note.
  • PDF size shown on the report screen once the PDF is made.

Version 1.2.4

12 Oct 2026
  • Pilot fixes from the briefing.
  • Elsewhere: a photo taken away from the alignment (site or third-party laboratory, factory, precast yard, another site) is set to Elsewhere with a place name instead of a chainage; places are remembered on the phone, a photo taken more than 150 m from the road offers it, and it no longer counts as "no chainage".
  • Progress % on each photo (an estimate, with quick buttons 25 / 50 / 75 / 100), printed with the status, e.g. In progress, 50% complete. The element box now names what to type for the activity: Sampling point (water, air and noise monitoring, sampling, instrumentation), Element (bridges, culverts, a structure), Layer / level (earthworks, pavement) or Element / point; a sampling point prints as Sampling point W1, and the PDF column reads Structure / element.
  • Report ref. no. on every report, e.g. WP12-WPC-SYA-20261012-1 (package, team, initials, date, report no. of the day on this phone), set from the initials in Report details and editable on the report screen; printed in the PDF header and footer, the WhatsApp text, the RFI photo record, the file names and the register.
  • Logos for each team (Setup → Report logos), e.g. JKR + WPC for WPC reports and JKR + CST for CST internal reports, as an image or a text; the check before sharing names the team.
  • HEIC / HEIF photos can be added on Android and laptops (converted on the phone; the converter downloads the first time it is needed), with their date and GPS.
  • Period report (experimental): Report type Period keeps collecting photos over several days and makes one report for the week or month: a progress table over the period (latest status and progress for each activity and place), then the photos day by day; the WhatsApp text is grouped by day.
  • Date taken for photos with no camera date (e.g. saved from WhatsApp): the date is read from the file name where it has one (e.g. IMG-20261010-WA0012.jpg), otherwise the file's date is used and flagged; the card shows a Date taken box to check or change it, Tag selected can set it for several photos, and a period report lists photos whose date is not confirmed or outside the period.
  • Test result for testing and sampling activities (Passed, Failed, Retest required, Pending test result, Pending official test result, or your own), printed with the status, e.g. Inspected, Result: Pending test result, and kept in the register (new CSV column Test result).
  • Remove selected photos in one step, with Undo.
  • Tidier screen: the photo on each card is smaller and the card is shorter (Structure beside the element box, Progress beside Status); New report is a red button at the top of the photo list and goes back to a Daily report; a report date that is not today shows in orange on the closed Report details line. The ZIP archive also holds report_data.json for compiling reports later. Register and CSV: new columns Place, Progress %, Report ref, Photo date (photos) and Report ref, Date to (reports), added at the end.

Version 1.2.3

11 Oct 2026
  • Pilot version.
  • Photos per page: choose 2 (larger) or 4 per A4 page, for the daily report and the RFI photo record.
  • Status for each photo (In progress, Completed, Ready for inspection, Inspected, Re-inspection required, Rectification required, Observation, or your own), printed in English and BM and kept in the register.
  • Level / element box moved onto the photo card (typed, e.g. Berm 3, Layer 2, P2).
  • RFI no. moved onto the photo card; the Generate screen shows a Make RFI photo records button at the top when photos have an RFI no.
  • Same as previous now copies remarks and status, and keeps a photo's own GPS chainage.
  • Check before you share lists photos with no activity or chainage, with buttons to go to each.
  • Send feedback on WhatsApp and User guide in Setup. User guide added as GUIDE.md, and a news post on the pilot in posts/. The app asks the browser to keep its data and reminds you to export the register after 7 days. A short notice shows what changed after each update.
  • Alignment diagram (experimental, off unless ticked): a line from the start to the end chainage with the zones and a marker for each photo (above the line LHS, on it CL, below it RHS), with the chainage range covered, on page 1 of the daily report and of each RFI photo record. Optional structure lines under the diagram, each ticked on its own: Bridges, Pipe culverts, Box culverts, Junction culverts and RS walls, with a tick and the structure no. at each drawing chainage.
  • Chainage to sits beside Chainage: tap + Chainage to for a stretch of work.
  • Your own zones in Setup: add, remove and rename zones (e.g. 5 zones for CST); a team setup update keeps them, and Use team zones goes back.
  • Sort by chainage beside Sort by time.
  • RFI inspection list (experimental, for WPC): the next-day RFI summary list, pasted from Excel (a chainage location goes into the chainage boxes) or added one by one, sorted by time or by RFI no., shared as an A4 landscape PDF, WhatsApp text or CSV, with Clear list and earlier lists to open or delete; RFI nos. on photo cards are suggested from the list and the RFI photo record takes its description. Setup → Experimental features shows or hides it.
  • See what changed link in the update notice and What changed in Setup.
  • RFI photo record simplified as an attachment to the RFI form: drawing reference, stage, result boxes and the SOR block removed; activities with BQ / QC refs, structure / level and a photo index (no., time, activity, location, level, status) added; sign-off by Prepared by (WPC) and Checked by (CST). Register and team sheet: the RFI Stage and Result columns replaced by Photo status. Fixed: summary table headings overlapping in bilingual reports; long header values (e.g. several zones) cut off, now shortened, e.g. CST Z1, Z3, Z4; zones listed in photo order instead of zone order; "BQ BQ" in the Register photo list. README: CST is the Consultant Supervision Team.

Version 1.2.2

10 Oct 2026
  • Activities with two or more items in one name are now separate activities, e.g. Prime coat and Tack coat; PDA test, PIT test and Sonic logging test; Incident and Near miss. Each has its own BQ ref where the BQ separates them (e.g. Close turfing – slope 7.5.1, road shoulder 7.5.2). The list now has 143 BQ-based activities and 50 QC tests. Names corrected: PVD installation (was PVD / PHD installation) and Hydromulching (was Hydromulching / hydroseeding). Fixed: the category tabs under the search box in the activity list sometimes did not show; they now stay at the top while scrolling the list. Long BQ refs in the list wrap instead of squeezing the activity name.

Version 1.2.1

10 Oct 2026
  • QC testing added as its own category: 48 contract QC tests (not in the BQ) from the Schedule of Testing, marked QC in the list, captions and reports, e.g. Cube test – G40 · QC. The BQ testing category is renamed QA testing & inspection. A photo can now have more than one activity (tap each in the list, then Done); bulk tagging and Copy previous carry all of them. Optional team register guide (TEAM_REGISTER.md) and its script (Code.gs) added to the repository; the script itself is unchanged.

Version 1.2

09 Oct 2026
  • Prepared for the pilot. ROADMAP.md added. RFI photo record: sign-off blocks changed to Prepared by (WPC), Checked by (CST) and Acknowledged by (SOR) (BM: Disediakan oleh, Disemak oleh, Diperakui oleh). Result heading reads Inspection (by CST). The RFI record footer reads "Generated by" so it does not clash with the Prepared by block. README updated to match.

Version 1.1

09 Oct 2026
  • First version published on GitHub. RFI photo record added: one PDF per RFI no., following the JKR Sabah RFI form, with result tick boxes, comments, sign-off blocks and photos. Register log added (Reports, RFIs, Photos; CSV export; import other users' logs; optional Google Sheet team register). Activity list extended to 120 items, including hydromulching (BQ 7.5.3), rock drilling & blasting, proof rolling, joint dipping and cube tests. Users can add activities in Setup. Zones Z1–Z4 set. Warning when a photo's chainage is outside the zone in the header. setup.json loads the project setup on every phone automatically.

Version 1.0

09 Oct 2026
  • First working version (tested internally, not published on GitHub): photo tagging by activity (BQ ref), structure, chainage, side, element, RFI no. and remarks. Chainage from GPS using the WP12 centreline. PDF report with 4 photos per page, captioned photos, WhatsApp summary text, ZIP archive. Captions in English, BM or both. Works offline.
